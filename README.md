## ☁️ SaaS / Hosted Video Platforms 💼

> **📊 Enterprise Video Platform Market Context**: The global enterprise video platform market is estimated at **~$8.0 Billion in 2026**, projected to reach **~$20.0 Billion by 2032**. The market is **highly fragmented** with over 50 competing providers across corporate video management, internal communications, sales enablement, and live event broadcasting. No single vendor holds a winner-take-all dominance, though Microsoft leads in workplace integration via Microsoft 365.

*Platforms below are sorted by company size (revenue / valuation descending).*
